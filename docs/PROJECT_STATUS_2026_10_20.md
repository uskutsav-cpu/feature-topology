The project is being completed before manuscript preparation. This is a dated
status record, not a claim of complete research or established high novelty.

## Limits on claims

The continuous-circle certificates concern specified class-sector midpoint
circles in four seed-zero saved models, interpreted as real-valued networks
with exact dyadic weights. Sixteen circles are not sixteen independent training
seeds. Same-implementation standalone replay is not an independent verifier or
a Lean proof. No full-torus, every-theta, machine-floating-point injectivity,
OOD robustness, or universally high-novelty claim follows.

The new verified separation evidence can support a precise distinction between
metric contraction and actual identification. The elementary separation lemma,
affine ReLU relaxation, and activation-vertex bounding principles are supporting
tools with prior art. Neither software test counts nor a larger experiment grid
establish scientific novelty. See the [novelty audit](NOVELTY_AUDIT_2026_10_20.md).
The relevance-sweep measurements concern the original phi factor; for positive
lambda it is no longer a pure nuisance. The
[coordinate audit](RELEVANCE_FIBER_SCOPE.md) explains that distinction without
relabeling the stored measurements as task-preserving fiber evaluations.
