# Research completion status, 2026-10-20

The project is being completed before manuscript preparation. This is a dated
status record, not a claim of complete research or established high novelty.

## Completed and checked

- All 845 unique requested synthetic production profiles were recovered and
  checked against archived release indices and member hashes.
- All 140 nuisance-shift evaluations, 140 VCI/conventional baseline evaluations,
  and 140 exact selected-checkpoint alias audits are retained.
- The recorded nested comparison across held-out training seeds finds no added
  predictive benefit from the fiber diagnostics. Spurious-shift prediction is
  worse in all five held-out seeds. The negative finding remains part of the study.
- Physical input audits pass for the synthetic studies, controlled rotated
  digits, dSprites, forty restricted rational-polygon certificates, and the
  compiled seventeen-lemma Lean audit. dSprites accuracy limitations and the
  negative width-scaling crossover gate remain visible.
- A portable additional comparison consumes only source-bound inputs inside
  the validation repository. Shared primary/pilot aliases are bound under both
  their logical and canonical paths in the freeze.
- The continuous-fiber feasibility screen retains all thirteen gamma values at
  seed zero: four eligible readouts and nine failed screens. Failed screens do
  not demonstrate noninjectivity.
- The first full-torus interval attempts and all sixteen V2 fixed-circle
  attempts were inconclusive and are retained. V3 produced sixteen candidates;
  thirteen passed separate-process replay and three failed the printed-bound
  check. See the [encoding incident](FIXED_FIBER_V3_ENCODING_INCIDENT.md).
- The exact-bound V4 implementation passes controls and a separate-process
  replay of the trained gamma 0.03125, theta = 3 pi/4 case. The full sixteen-case
  hosted generation/replay campaign is running; its outcome is not assumed.

## Limits on claims

The continuous-circle certificates concern specified class-sector midpoint
circles in four seed-zero saved models, interpreted as real-valued networks
with exact dyadic weights. Sixteen circles are not sixteen independent training
seeds. Same-implementation standalone replay is not an independent verifier or
a Lean proof. No full-torus, every-theta, machine-floating-point injectivity,
OOD robustness, or universally high-novelty claim follows.

The new verified separation evidence can support a precise distinction between
metric contraction and actual identification. The elementary separation lemma,
affine ReLU relaxation, and activation-vertex bounding principles are supporting
tools with prior art. Neither software test counts nor a larger experiment grid
establish scientific novelty. See the [novelty audit](NOVELTY_AUDIT_2026_10_20.md).
The relevance-sweep measurements concern the original phi factor; for positive
lambda it is no longer a pure nuisance. The
[coordinate audit](RELEVANCE_FIBER_SCOPE.md) explains that distinction without
relabeling the stored measurements as task-preserving fiber evaluations.
