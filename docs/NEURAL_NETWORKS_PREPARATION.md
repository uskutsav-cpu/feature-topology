## Interpretation constraints

- The completed width-scaling gate returns **crossover**. A failed transition test
  is retained as a research result, not retuned away.
- Sampled noncollision, small margins, decoding failure, and PH changes do not
  certify a continuum task quotient or global injectivity.
- Weak dSprites classification and target-unreached CIFAR calibration remain
  visible. Experiment completion is separate from successful validation.
- Training seeds are the independent statistical replicates. PH subsamples and
  noise repetitions do not increase the seed count.
- Existing completed results are reused without overwriting or rerunning them.
